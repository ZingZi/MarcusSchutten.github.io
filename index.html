"How and why do you stay OPTIMISTIC in a tough situation?"

Being optimistic or pessimistic is a choice you make every day. By being in control of my emotions I am able to see an opportunity in a objectively negative situation. It required constant attention but by not being affected negatively by a minor or major inconveniance, I am able to move on through the day with a possitive attitude.

Often, a "tough" situation is only tough due to the way you react to it.

How does being OPEN-MINDED show in your life?

With technological advancement and politics, tomorrow can be vastely different compared to today. I have noticed the times I am more close-minded my overall wellbeing is compromised. So for me, staying open-minded is necessary. I enjoy learning, and for me to continue doing so I need to have an open-mind. Furthermore, in friendships, relations, and teamworking, I try my best to be open-minded make everyone feel heard and seen. Everyones opinion matters and deserves to be heard.

When we stop being close-minded, what we don't know becomes larger than what we do know.

How do you stay MOTIVATED?

Staying motivated is a constant battle. I used to be very unambitious but as I saw people around me succeeding I started to get envious. Connecting it to my other trait of being open-minded, I stopped being envious and instead used my desire to be like them to motivate myself. Since then my motivation has become something more deep rooted of being the best version of myself that can help others and be an role model to others.

You only got one life, might as well make the most of it.
