Line Operator

Fresh Servant Oy Ab

Duration: 9 Months

After graduating from upper secondary school I got hired by Fresh Servant Oy Ab. During the nine month period, I worked within the final product manufacturing of their products. Tre months in I got the opportunity to be trained as a line operator. I learned to handle the machines and fix the problems that occurred, this came with noticeable more responsibility.

I worked between 3-5 days a week, mostly 5, with morning or evening shifts eight hours each. Often one out of the five work days was on the weekend.

My work experience at Fresh was very enjoyable and educative. I gained a much larger knowledge of how companies operate and what it takes to keep every process running.
